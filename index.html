Who we are

Faith, put to work.

For The City is a faith-based nonprofit that partners with churches to meet the needs of struggling people in Costa Rica — spiritually, physically, and relationally. We believe showing up consistently, in small and practical ways, is how trust gets built and lives get changed.

We work alongside our home church, Iglesia de la Ciudad, and welcome mission teams from churches across the U.S. who want to serve alongside us for a week, a season, or longer.

Our work

Six ways we show up for our neighbors.

Every program starts with the same question: what does this person need right now? The answers led us here.

Food Pantry & Night Outreach

Our team packs and delivers meals to families across the city each week, including a nighttime outreach that brings hot food directly to men and women living on the street.

English Classes

Free English classes open doors — to better jobs, further education, and bigger opportunities for the people we walk alongside.

Dental Program

Visiting dental teams provide checkups, cleanings, and procedures that would otherwise be completely out of reach for the families we serve.

A Place to Stay

A small home on our property gives a handful of people a safe, stable place to live while they recover and get back on their feet.

Health Services

From basic checkups to specialist referrals, we help connect families with the healthcare they need but can't always access on their own.

Education & Discipleship

Bible studies, tutoring, and mentoring walk alongside people for the long haul — not just a single visit, but a relationship that keeps going.

Where it started
"It started the way a lot of good things do — someone noticed a need close to home, and did something about it."
The idea behind For The City

Our founder was inspired by Willow Creek Community Church outside Chicago, where one man began handing out groceries from the trunk of his car to families in need — and it grew into a full care center serving the whole community.

That same conviction is what's behind Cornerstone Church's own outreach in Bluffton, South Carolina. For The City brings it to San José: showing up for our neighbors in ordinary, practical ways, and trusting that faithfulness in small things adds up to something bigger.

Our home in Costa Rica

Iglesia de la Ciudad is where the work happens.

Every program runs out of our home church, Iglesia de la Ciudad, in San José. It's where meals get packed, where dental clinics are set up, where English classes meet — and where visiting mission teams roll up their sleeves alongside our staff.

Teams have come from churches across the U.S. to mix concrete, serve meals, and build relationships that outlast the trip. If your church is looking for a place to serve together, we'd love to host you.

— The For The City team
