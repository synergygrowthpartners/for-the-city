How We Help

Six ways we show up for our neighbors

Every program starts with the same question: what does this person need right now?

01

Food Pantry & Night Outreach

02

03

Dental Program

Where It Started
"It started the way a lot of good things do — someone noticed a need close to home, and did something about it."
The Idea Behind For The City

Our founder was inspired by Willow Creek Community Church outside Chicago, where one man began handing out groceries from the trunk of his car — and it grew into a full care center serving the whole community.

That same conviction is behind Cornerstone Church's own outreach in Bluffton, South Carolina. For The City brings it to San José: showing up for our neighbors in ordinary, practical ways.

Our Home in Costa Rica

Iglesia de la Ciudad is where the work happens.

Every program runs out of our home church in San José — meals get packed, dental clinics are set up, English classes meet, and visiting mission teams roll up their sleeves alongside our staff.

Teams have come from churches across the U.S. to mix concrete, serve meals, and build relationships that outlast the trip.
